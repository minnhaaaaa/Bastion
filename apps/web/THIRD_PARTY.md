# Wenvy frontend provenance

Reference: https://github.com/ACM-VIT/wenvy

Source revision: `0e6753b67688e0309f7b1d79b26a9c18cb74612d` (`apps/landing`).

The Wenvy landing stylesheet is preserved in `src/wenvy.css`. Its Koi, Logo, Reveal, and Envelope components are adapted in `src/components/wenvy`. The Bastion masthead and terminal use Wenvy's component structure, with Bastion routes and persisted backend records. Landing layout, font families, palette, grain, wordmark texture, section bands, and motion follow that source.

Adaptations replace Wenvy branding and secret-management content, add reduced-motion handling and mobile navigation, and retain Bastion's backend-driven console and Arena. The scripted terminal demo from the source is not used; no demo events or workflow records are bundled.
