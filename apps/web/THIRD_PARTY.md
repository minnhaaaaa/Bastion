# Third-party assets

The earlier Wenvy-derived frontend (styles, Koi/Envelope/Masthead/Logo components) and the Originkit components have been removed. The current design, emblem and illustrations are original to Bastion.

- Fonts: Archivo, Instrument Sans, JetBrains Mono (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License).
- Animation: GSAP and its plugins (GreenSock standard license; all plugins free since 3.13).
- Transitions: Barba.js (MIT).
